When your deploy fails or your API calls start timing out, the first question is: **is it us, or is it them?** Vendor status pages often turn red only after the outage is well underway, so we built our own answer.

**[upti.my/status](https://www.upti.my/status?utm_source=github&utm_medium=release&utm_campaign=is-it-down)** shows the live status of 25 services developers depend on, based on our own checks from multiple regions, independent of what the vendor reports.

## What's on each page

- **A live verdict** that refreshes every minute: operational, degraded or down
- **Status per component**, so you can see whether it's the website, the API or something like Git operations that's failing
- **Response-time charts** with p50 and p95 latency
- **Outage history** with when each incident started and ended
- **Share buttons** to post the current status to your team or on X

## Get alerted when a dependency goes down

If you use Uptimy, you can **follow** any of these services from **Dependencies** in the app. When one goes down, an incident opens in your workspace and your alerting fires, just like for your own monitors. Following doesn't count against your monitor quota. [Read the docs →](https://www.upti.my/docs/dependencies?utm_source=github&utm_medium=release)
