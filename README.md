This is the home of **Uptimy** on GitHub: product releases, live status badges and links to everything else. Uptimy itself is a hosted service at [upti.my](https://www.upti.my/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme).

## Stay up to date

- **Watch → Custom → Releases** on this repository to get every launch in your GitHub notifications.
- Or subscribe to the feed: [`releases.atom`](https://github.com/uptimy/uptimy/releases.atom)

## What Uptimy does

- **[Uptime monitoring](https://www.upti.my/uptime-monitoring?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme)** for websites and APIs, including SSL, DNS, TCP, gRPC and GraphQL checks from multiple regions
- **[Cron job & heartbeat monitoring](https://www.upti.my/api-monitoring/cron-jobs?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme)** for scheduled jobs, backups and workers
- **[Webhook](https://www.upti.my/webhook-monitoring?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme)** and **[workflow monitoring](https://www.upti.my/workflow-monitoring?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme)** with payload and data validation
- **[Incident management](https://www.upti.my/incident-management?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme)** and **[alert routing](https://www.upti.my/alert-routing?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme)** with escalation and deduplication
- **[Status pages](https://www.upti.my/status-pages?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme)** that update from your monitors
